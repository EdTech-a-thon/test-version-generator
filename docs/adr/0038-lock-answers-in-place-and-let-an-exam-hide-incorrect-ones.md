# Lock answers in place, and let an Exam hide incorrect ones

Shuffling answers, by Vary or in export Versions (ADR-0028), moved every Multiple Choice answer, so “All of the above” could print as A and “None of these” in the middle. And teachers who keep a long list of distractors on a Question wanted a test to show only some of them, a different few on each Version, without editing the Question.

**A Locked Answer keeps its authored position.** It is Question Content, because it is about what the answer means, so it lives on the answer in the Question Bank and travels in the Question Bank Record. Any Multiple Choice answer may be locked, in a Multiple Choice Question or a Multiple Choice Part, the correct one included; the question editor shows a lock at the right of each answer row on hover, and always on a locked one, and the Working Copy's sheet and the Question Bank's reading mark one with a small lock beside the correct-answer check. Shuffling keeps every locked answer at its index and shuffles the others among the indexes left, so a question with fewer than two answers free to move does not Vary and offers no Versions of its answers. An answer order stored before an answer was locked, or one an Exam Record carries, is read with each locked answer put back at its index, so nothing has to be migrated. True/False never moves, so its answers carry no lock. A Matching Word Bank cannot be locked for now: its letters are what Items name by identity, and nobody has asked; it can follow the same rule later.

An answer worded like “All of the above”, “None of these”, “Both A and B”, “A and C only” or “Neither A nor B” is locked by its wording. The match ignores case, punctuation and brackets, wants a quantifier with something that points at the other answers (a bare “All” or “None” is an ordinary answer), or two or more letters A–H joined by and, or or nor; Roman numerals are left alone, since “I and II only” names statements in the stem and may move. **Detection is read, not run.** A choice stores the teacher's decision — locked, unlocked, or none — and one with none is locked exactly when its words read that way now. So a freshly typed, imported, duplicated or pasted answer needs no hook to detect it; retyping “All of the above” into an ordinary answer unlocks it; and an answer the teacher unlocked stays unlocked however it is reworded, as one they locked stays locked. We rejected locking on creation and edit and storing the result, because an edit hook must then tell a teacher's unlock from a detection it should redo, and every import path — Question Files, assistant packages, older records — would need it too. The cost is that changing the wording rules could change an existing Question's undecided answers; Export Records keep the plans they printed, so nothing already exported moves.

**Question Bank Record 0.9.0** adds an optional boolean `locked` to a Multiple Choice Question's choice and a Multiple Choice Part's choice; a True/False choice must not carry it. Test Parrot writes `true` for every answer it treats as locked, whatever locked it, so no consumer needs our reading of the wording; `false` only for one the teacher unlocked, so an importer that locks by wording leaves it alone; and nothing otherwise. An absent `locked` makes no decision, which is how Test Parrot reads every choice of a `0.1.0`–`0.8.0` record, a Question File's and an assistant's package, so their “All of the above” is locked on import. It is a minor version rather than a patch because an older consumer would shuffle a locked answer, changing what it means. Assistants keep writing `0.7.0`, and the wording does the rest.

**A Hidden Answer is an incorrect answer an Exam position leaves off.** It is Exam presentation, like answer order and answer columns: the Question keeps every answer, and a position chooses how many of its incorrect answers show, from one to all, all by default. The Working Copy stores, per Multiple Choice Question, the ids it hides, beside `choiceOrder`; the count is what is left. Like an order, the list is tolerated rather than validated, and the rules are applied every time it is read: the correct answer and every Locked Answer always show, at least one incorrect answer shows, and nothing hides while no answer is marked correct — there would be no telling a distractor from the answer — or beside a Locked Answer that names others by letter, whose letters hiding would change. The sheet says so under the answers: “Showing 3 of 5 incorrect answers · 2 hidden”, or why every answer shows. The answers shown keep the arrangement's order and close up, lettered as they print, so a Locked Answer last stays last and the Answer Key's letter is the printed one. Multiple Choice Parts do not hide answers yet: their presentation is set per Part, which this menu does not reach, and Exam Records do not carry a Part's answer order either.

The question's context menu gains a **Vary answers** submenu in place of *Shuffle answer order*: *Shuffle answers*, then *Show all N incorrect answers* down to *Show 1 of N incorrect*, for a Multiple Choice question that can hide any. Both act on the selection when the question is in it; a count is clamped to what each question allows, and *all* means all of each. Choosing a smaller count hides more at random from those showing; a larger one brings hidden ones back at random, so what was showing stays. *Shuffle answers* keeps the count and draws again which incorrect answers show as well as their order, and redraws until what a student sees has changed. An export Version that shuffles answers does the same for itself, so Versions differ in their distractors too, and how many Versions an Exam allows counts every distinct set and order shown; one that does not shuffle answers keeps the Working Copy's hidden answers. A Version's Layout Plans already hold exactly the answers it printed, so its Export Record reprints them with no seed.

**Exam Record 0.4.0** adds a Multiple Choice position's optional `hiddenAnswers`, the record's choice ids it leaves off. Import rejects an id that is not one of the Question's answers, and its correct answer; a Locked Answer named there is shown anyway. Another branch is adding `margins` and `questionStyle` to Exam Record 0.4.0; `hiddenAnswers` is additive to the same version, so the two merge as a union of members. An Exam PDF's package still carries the Working Copy's arrangement alone (ADR-0028), now with its hidden answers.
