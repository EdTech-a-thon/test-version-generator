# Make a Stimulus with its Parts one Question

A teacher sent a Regents-style block: a passage about the Ottoman Empire's decline with its source line, followed by two Multiple Choice questions a student answers from it. The source tests they are converting are full of these — a passage, a quote, a diagram or a table, then questions that are meaningless without it. Today there is nowhere to put one; `/extract` sends it to Unconverted Questions.

`'stimulus'` is therefore a fifth Question Type, last in Section order. One Question of it holds a Stimulus — free rich text, with any source line typed as ordinary content rather than a field of its own — and an ordered list of Parts, each either Multiple Choice or Short Answer. Modelling the Stimulus as a shared record that ordinary Questions point to was rejected for the reason ADR 0021 gave for Matching: the Stimulus is what makes the Parts one thing, and an Exam holding a Part without its Stimulus would print a question nobody can answer. The Question Bank keeps it whole, and an Exam adds, Removes and Replaces it as one Question. True/False and Matching Parts, and a Stimulus inside a Stimulus, were left out: nobody has asked for them, and a whole Matching set inside a Part has no sensible layout. A True/False Part could be added later without disturbing anything here.

On the test, a Stimulus takes one number, and its Parts print lettered beneath it — `12.` then `a.`, `b.` — rather than one number per Part as Matching does. It prints in its own Stimulus section because its Parts mix types and a Section is one type; the section's directions are generic. Parts never shuffle, since they are lettered in place and often build on one another; Vary moves a whole Stimulus among the others in its section, and shuffles each Multiple Choice Part's answers by stable choice id as it does a Multiple Choice question's. Answer columns and Work Space are set per Part on the exam sheet, exactly as they are per question, and a Short Answer Part without Work Space gets a Short Answer question's default.

Difficulty and Topics belong to the Stimulus, because the bank finds Questions and a Part is not one. The Answer Key gives the Stimulus one entry under its number with a line per Part: a letter for a Multiple Choice Part, the Suggested Answer for a Short Answer Part. A Stimulus with no Parts, like a Multiple Choice question with no correct answer, is incomplete rather than invalid.

The editor lays a Stimulus out as the paper does, with as little nesting as it can. The Stimulus is written unnested at the top, where a question's stem is. Below it a "Parts" heading, ruled full width, stands over every Part; each Part carries a tag with its letter and type (`a · Multiple Choice`), with controls to move it up or down and to delete it, then one box that reads as a question of its type: its stem as the top cell, joined to the existing Multiple Choice answers or Suggested Answer component beneath it. No box surrounds a Part or the Parts. A Part's type is a menu on its tag, and switches between Multiple Choice and Short Answer only while its answers are blank, so a switch never discards work; the stem stays. A new Stimulus starts with one blank Multiple Choice Part, and "Add Part" after the last Part offers Multiple Choice or Short Answer. Deleting the last Part leaves only the heading and "Add Part"; the Parts node and each Part's answers component regrow if deleted, as a True/False pair and a Matching set do. The bank lists a Stimulus by its first line and its count of Parts, and search matches text in any Part.

The Stimulus and Part a are kept together on a page where they fit. After that the plan breaks only between Parts, never inside one, and a Stimulus taller than a page may split between its blocks as a last resort. No "(continued)" cue is printed.

The exchange format follows the rule ADRs 0020 and 0021 followed: a `0.3.0` consumer must reject an unknown Question Type, so the Question Bank Record becomes `0.4.0`, published beside the earlier versions. A record Stimulus carries the Stimulus as its `stem`, as every record Question does, and `parts`, each `{id, type, stem}` plus `choices` for a Multiple Choice Part or an optional `suggestedAnswer` for a Short Answer Part; Part ids are `qN-sM` and their choices `qN-sM-cK`, clear of the ids Multiple Choice and Matching already use. Part columns stay out of the record, as a question's do. The Exam package does not yet carry per-Part presentation — a Part's answer order, columns and Work Space — so an Exam imported from one gives every Part its defaults. `/extract` converts a "Base your answers to questions 12 and 13 on…" block into one Stimulus Question even though the source numbers each Part, and it leaves a block containing a Part of any other type in Unconverted Questions rather than forcing it.
