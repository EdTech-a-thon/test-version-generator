# Organize Home around editable resources

Home is a resume surface for mutable resources rather than a global export feed. It shows parallel, horizontally scrolling Recent Exams and Recently Updated Question Banks sections with New and View all actions; `/exams` and `/question-banks` provide searchable full collections. Exams are ordered by when they were last opened, while Question Banks are ordered by their latest substantive update and display that time; creation, rename, and Question creation, duplication, editing, or deletion update a bank, while opening, filtering, Exam composition, and export do not. Editing a Question through an Exam still updates its owning bank. Exam cards preview the current Working Copy and identify unsaved changes, while Question Bank cards summarize Question count, Topics, and the number of current Exams using the bank. Individual Question usage appears in the Question editor and destructive deletion confirmation, but not on compact rows in the editor's Question Bank panel; Home can also list a bank's using Exams. Export History remains reachable only through its owning Exam.
