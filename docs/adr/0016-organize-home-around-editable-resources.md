# Organize Home around editable resources

Home is a resume surface for mutable resources rather than a global export feed. Every surface outside the editor wears one dashboard shell: a persistent left navigation naming Home, Exams, Question Banks and the Editor, breadcrumbs across the top, and the local-storage explanation as a top-bar affordance rather than a banner Home has to carry. Home itself shows a horizontally scrolling shelf of recent Exams and the complete grid of Question Banks, each under a quiet label rather than a title, because navigation is the left nav's job and Home should not restate it; `/exams` and `/question-banks` provide the searchable full collections and own creating and importing. Exams are ordered by when they were last opened, while Question Banks are ordered by their latest substantive update and display that time; creation, rename, and Question creation, duplication, editing, or deletion update a bank, while opening, filtering, Exam composition, and export do not. Editing a Question through an Exam still updates its owning bank. An Exam card is that Exam's own first page drawn at printed geometry and scaled down, so a card and a sheet cannot drift apart, and it identifies unsaved changes. A Question Bank card summarizes Question count, Topics, and how many Exams currently use the bank, and keeps its actions behind one per-card menu rather than bolting controls to the tile. Which Exams use a bank, like individual Question usage, appears in the Question editor and the destructive deletion confirmation — where the teacher is deciding whether to change or delete something — rather than on a card or on compact rows in the editor's Question Bank panel. Export History remains reachable only through its owning Exam.
